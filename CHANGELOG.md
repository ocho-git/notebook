## 0.1.0 — 5 November 2026

Initial standalone extraction of Zen Garden's animated SceneKit field-guide book.

- SwiftUI wrapper and per-instance controller, document, leaf and page APIs.
- Custom cover title/colors, lighting, edge placement and host-controlled rotation.
- Animated cover, folding page geometry, touch gestures and riffling navigation.
- Opt-in procedural page sounds and bounded on-demand page texture loading.
- Checked-in iOS demo project, integration/architecture guides and assistant instructions.
- Portable geometry, spring and audio tests; unsigned generic iOS and Simulator build checks.
